Starlink helped rescue teams bring 211 stranded tourists to safety in Argentina’s remote mountains. ❤️🇦🇷

A snowstorm trapped dozens of vehicles near Balcón del Pissis, Catamarca. Snow blocked the roads, leaving tourists stranded in freezing conditions.

Satellite phones and @Starlink helped raise the alarm. Telecentro also supplied Starlink connectivity to strengthen communications between teams working in the mountains, where conventional networks were limited.

Firefighters, police, medical teams and local rescuers joined the operation as heavy machinery cleared paths through the snow.

Authorities confirmed 211 people were rescued, with 39 sheltered at a mining camp until they could descend safely.

A life-saving effort on the ground, supported by connectivity from space.
