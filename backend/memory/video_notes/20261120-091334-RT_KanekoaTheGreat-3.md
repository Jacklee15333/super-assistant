Elon Musk（埃隆·马斯克）揭示了 Starlink（星链）如何拯救乌克兰免于崩溃，并就有关他限制星链服务的指控澄清事实：

“没有任何一个私人个体比我为乌克兰提供的帮助更多。我确保星链在俄罗斯入侵之前就已经提前部署在乌克兰。”

“俄罗斯入侵时，他们瘫痪了所有通信设施、所有移动通信基站、所有光纤线路，以及每一个卫星通信系统。只有一个系统是他们无法瘫痪的，那就是星链。”

“如果他们没有星链，他们的整条防线就会崩溃，因为他们没有通信系统，而俄罗斯就会获胜。那么，在你看来，这听起来像是亲俄行为吗？”

“即使到了今天，星链仍然是乌克兰军方乃至整个乌克兰通信体系的支柱。如果我们关闭星链，乌克兰的整个前线就会立即崩溃。”

“从一开始，我就说过，星链是一个民用系统。它并不是用来发动造成他人死亡的进攻性无人机袭击的。如果你想这么做，就需要使用 Starshield（星盾），那是一个由美国政府控制的独立系统。”

“星链系统中设有速度限制。这个速度上限与乌克兰道路上的最高限速一致，我记得大约是每小时80英里。”

“不要要求我以私人个体的身份作出杀戮决定，因为这些决定有时不仅会导致士兵死亡，还会造成无辜者丧生。作出这种决定并不是我的职责。然而，我们一直被无休止地施压，要求我们这么做，我认为这简直令人无法接受。”

“这些规则从一开始就已经存在，并且实施了很多年。现在人们向我施压，要求我改变这些规则，但我不会改变。”

Elon Musk reveals how Starlink saved Ukraine from collapse, and sets the record straight on claims he throttled it:

“There is no private individual who has helped Ukraine more than I have. I made sure that Starlink was in Ukraine, prepositioned ahead of the Russian invasion.”

“When Russia invaded, they disabled all communications, all cell towers, all fiber cables, and every satellite communication system. There was one system they could not disable, and that was Starlink.”

“If they had not had Starlink, their entire line would have collapsed because they had no communication system, and Russia would have won. Now, does that sound like a pro-Russian action to you?”

“Even to this present day, Starlink provides the backbone of communications for the Ukrainian military and for Ukraine more broadly. If we were to turn off Starlink, the entire Ukrainian front would collapse immediately.”

“From the beginning, I said that Starlink is a civilian system. It is not meant to be used for offensive drone strikes that kill people. If you want to do that, you need to do Starshield, which is a separate system that is controlled by the US government.”

“In the Starlink system, there is a speed limit. That speed limit is the speed of the fastest road speed limit in Ukraine, which I think is about 80 miles an hour.”

“Do not ask me as a private individual to make kill decisions that sometimes result in the loss of innocent lives, not just soldiers. That is not my position to do. And yet we’ve been pressed relentlessly to do that, which I think is outrageous.”

“Those are the rules that have been in place since the beginning, for years. Now people pressure me to change it, but I’m not changing it.”
