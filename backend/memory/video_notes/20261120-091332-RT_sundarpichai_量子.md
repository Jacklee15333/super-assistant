### 🗣 博主原话

Entanglement usually gets top billing in quantum computing, but quantum contextuality, the idea that properties don't have fixed values independent of how you measure them, may be the deeper reason quantum computers can outperform classical ones.

Today on the cover of @ScienceMagazine, we put contextuality to work on our Willow quantum processor:

- won the "impossible" magic square game 98.3% of the time (classical limit: 88.9%)
- measured a 5.59 on state-independent contextuality tests (classical bound: 4, quantum max: 6)
- beat classical strategies in multiplayer games up to 71 qubits
- ran a landmark shallow-circuit algorithm at scale for the first time - up to 105 qubits

These tasks also catch subtle hardware errors random benchmarks miss, a sharper ruler for building better quantum systems. https://x.com/ScienceMagazine/status/2123492766214668527/photo/1
