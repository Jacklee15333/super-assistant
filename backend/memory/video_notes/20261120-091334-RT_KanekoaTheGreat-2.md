Elon Musk says Neuralink’s next product, Blindsight, will enable people who are completely blind to see:

“Our next product is what we call Blindsight.”

“Blindsight is a neural implant that interfaces directly with the visual cortex and enables people who are completely blind to see.”

“You’re wearing glasses or something like that that have a camera. That camera is transmitting to the Neuralink device that then transfers the signals to your visual cortex.”

“Enabling someone who is completely blind, meaning blind in both eyes or lost the optic nerve, potentially someone who has been blind from birth, to see.”
