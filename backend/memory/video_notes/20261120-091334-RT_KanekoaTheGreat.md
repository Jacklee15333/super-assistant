Elon Musk（埃隆·马斯克）表示，SpaceX（太空探索技术公司）的 StarMind（星智）轨道 AI 卫星，相比 Starlink V3（第三代星链卫星），是一个“容易得多”的工程问题：

“我不太明白为什么人们认为制造 AI 卫星会很困难，因为 SpaceX 已经有超过 11,000 颗卫星在轨运行，我认为这个数量是世界其他国家和地区卫星总数的两倍。”

“StarMind 卫星是我们整个计算系统的名称。举例来说，它比 Starlink V3 通信卫星更简单，而不是更复杂，后者是一个极其复杂的轨道无线电站。”

“它配备了有史以来制造出的最先进的相控阵天线，远比任何军方不惜任何代价所能拥有的天线更加先进。”

“相比之下，AI 卫星要容易得多，因为它实际上只是由一个非常庞大的太阳能电池板阵列和一个散热器组成。”

“太阳能电池板的面积大约是散热器面积的五倍。这只是基本的物理原理。”

“然后，在中心位置有一台星载计算机，其物理尺寸大约只有 Vera Rubin NVL（薇拉·鲁宾 NVL）机架的四分之一，却具备 Vera Rubin NVL72 的全部能力。”

“无论我怎么强调都不为过，StarMind 卫星的技术难度远低于 Starlink V3 通信卫星。我并不是说它轻而易举，但它没有我们已经在做的事情那么困难。”

Elon Musk says SpaceX’s StarMind orbital AI satellite is a “much easier” engineering problem than Starlink V3:

“I’m not sure why people think that making an AI satellite would be hard, given that SpaceX already has over 11,000 satellites in orbit, which is, I think, twice as much as the rest of the world combined.”

“The StarMind satellite, which is the name for our overall compute system, is simpler, not harder than, say, the Starlink V3 communication satellite, which is an incredibly complex orbital radio station.”

“It’s got the most advanced phased array antennas that have ever been made, far more advanced than anything that any military has at any price.”

“Compared to that, the AI satellite is much, much easier, because it really just consists of a very large array of solar panels and a radiator.”

“You’re gonna have about five times as much solar panel area as you will have radiator area. And that’s just basic physics.”

“Then you have the star computer at the center, which would be physically about a quarter of the size of, say, a Vera Rubin NVL rack while having all of the capability of a Vera Rubin NVL72.”

“I can’t emphasize enough that the StarMind satellite is a much easier technical problem than Starlink V3 communication satellite. I’m not saying it’s trivial, but it’s not as hard as what we’re already doing.”
