Transformers have barely changed since the 1890s: copper coils in a tank of oil.

Batteries, solar and compute run on DC. The grid runs on AC. Bridging the two has meant inverters plus transformers.

Powercore is a solid-state transformer, breaker and inverter in one box. Finally, one conversion and about half the losses.

Hats off to the Tesla power electronics engineers!
