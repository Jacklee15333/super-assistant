Back in 2016, Elon Musk laid out the scale of what it would actually take to build a self-sustaining city on Mars

The goal was to build a civilization that can survive on its own

The scale Elon laid out:

• ~100 people per ship  
• Mars launch opportunities roughly every 2 years
• Around 10,000 trips to move 1 million people  
• A spacecraft large enough to carry people, luggage and massive amounts of cargo  

At the time, the hardest part was obvious:

You needed a transportation system that can fly again and again

Now that future is getting much closer

Starship has returned from space and been caught by the tower

The next step is rapid reuse, reflying the same ship again and again until spaceflight starts looking more like aviation

In 2016, Elon described the transportation system needed to build a city on Mars

In 2026, SpaceX is finally starting to prove the reusability that makes it possible
