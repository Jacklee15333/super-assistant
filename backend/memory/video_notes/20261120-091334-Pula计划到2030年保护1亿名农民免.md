Pula aims to protect 100 million farmers from climate shocks by 2030. Climate modeling powered by NVIDIA Earth-2 will help it pursue that goal. 🌱

At The Earthshot Prize’s Impact Assembly in Mumbai, 2026 Finalist Pula announced it is joining NVIDIA Inception and will use Earth-2 to better understand how drought and extreme weather will affect the smallholder farmers it serves.

Faster, more detailed forecasts, alongside new support from AWS, will help Pula scale protection for farmers’ families and livelihoods.
