Elon Musk just shared an ambitious timeline for Terafab, which is being designed to become the largest building on Earth at 100 million square feet

And the first test chip is targeted for June 2027

"Terafab is still in its infancy, obviously. We only announced it earlier this year, but we've already recruited quite a strong team, and we're looking for more great engineers to join the Terafab project. It is one of the most exciting projects in the world: to create the largest, most advanced chip fabrication facility on Earth. In fact, it'll be the largest building of any kind on Earth. We expect it to ultimately be 100 million square feet, densely packed with the most advanced equipment in the world

We'll use relatively standard equipment at first, but then we'll be pushing suppliers to do special versions of the machines. In some cases, we may make our own machine if we feel that has to be done in order to improve the production process

So yes, I think it's going quite well. We expect to have the research fab in operation and hopefully make our first test chip around June of next year. So I guess we're about eight or nine months away from that. And if all goes well, we will achieve volume production towards the end of 2028, at least for phase one of Terafab. And then we'll go to phase two and onwards. But I think you'll see some very rapid evolution in the way that chips are made"
