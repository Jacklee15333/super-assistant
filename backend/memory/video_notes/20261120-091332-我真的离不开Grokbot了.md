I honestly can't live without Grokbot anymore -- I officially just upgraded to $200/m

It's actually the best AI assistant out there that can do anything.

I know a lot of people are just talking, so let me tell you exactly what I use it for (steal my bots below too for free)

- my chief of staff bot checks both my gmails every hour, drafts every brand deal reply for me to approve, and pings me with exactly what's owed

- my content bot scouts viral ai posts overnight and writes my typefully drafts so i wake up with ideas ready to film

- my job search bot applied to 5 jobs in under 7 minutes with a custom resume and cover letter for each one

- my coding agents run on my mac studio while i'm out so that any build that takes hours could start even if I'm not home, and my chief of staff pauses the heavy jobs whenever i go live

- my flipper bot prices my stuff off real sold comps and writes the listings

- my reservations bot watches resy for hard-to-get nyc tables and books them the second they drop

- my bills, claims and privacy bots shop my bills for savings, hunt unclaimed money and scrub my info off people-search sites

- my clipfarm bot handles customer emails and support directly through its own Grok email

All managed by a really pretty iOS app as well!
