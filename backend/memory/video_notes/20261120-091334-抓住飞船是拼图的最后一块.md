Elon Musk on why the historic Starship catch is a much bigger milestone than most people realize

“Catching the ship was the final major piece”

SpaceX has now achieved:

• 3 successful Super Heavy booster catches
• A booster that was reflown and caught again
• The FIRST EVER Starship upper-stage catch
• Ship 42 returning from orbit in good enough condition to be reflown with only minor repairs

For the first time, SpaceX has demonstrated that both stages of Starship can return and be recovered by the launch tower

And Elon confirmed that Ship 42 will likely be preserved for public display because of its historic achievement

“No one has ever taken a stage like that and created a truly reusable upper stage and certainly not brought it back to land propulsively while being caught by giant chopsticks”

Now look at what full and rapid reusability is expected to unlock:

• Up to 100 TIMES lower cost of accessing space
• The potential to deliver MILLIONS OF TONS of payload to orbit annually
• A transportation system where both stages return, refuel, and fly again

That's the scale of what SpaceX is building

Starship has already demonstrated the recovery of both stages. The next step is turning recovery into routine, rapid reuse

And that's what will fundamentally change the economics of spaceflight
