早在2015年，Elon Musk（埃隆·马斯克）就在Seattle（西雅图）启用了SpaceX（太空探索技术公司）的新卫星工程办公室，并怀揣着一个宏大的抱负：

“我们希望像革新火箭领域一样，彻底革新卫星领域。”

11年后，看看这一愿景已经变成了怎样的现实

• 超过11,100颗Starlink（星链）卫星在轨运行
• 网络连接覆盖174个国家和市场
• 1,360万个人用户
• 此外还有数十万企业客户
• 超过3,800架飞机已经配备星链服务
• 卫星直连手机服务已在约30个国家投入运营

而这些不仅仅是数字

星链正在为生活在光纤网络远远无法覆盖地区的家庭提供网络连接，为偏远村庄的学校接入互联网，让身处大洋中央的船只保持网络连接，并让数百万航空旅客在距离地面数千米的高空享受高速互联网

它还在帮助挽救生命

从营救被困在偏远荒野中的人员，到自然灾害发生后恢复应急通信，星链正在覆盖传统通信网络根本无法触及的地方

最初，马斯克只是站在西雅图的舞台上，描述自己对全球卫星网络的愿景，而如今，这一愿景已经成为人类有史以来建造的最大规模的卫星星座

Back in 2015, Elon Musk opened SpaceX's new satellite engineering office in Seattle with an enormous ambition:

“We want to revolutionize the satellite side of things just as we've done with the rocket side of things.”

Eleven years later, look at what that vision has become

• 11,100+ Starlink satellites in orbit
• Connectivity across 174 countries and markets
• 13.6 MILLION consumer subscribers
• Hundreds of thousands of additional enterprise customers
• 3,800+ aircraft already equipped with Starlink
• Satellite-to-phone connectivity operating across roughly 30 countries

And these aren't just numbers

Starlink is connecting families living far beyond the reach of fiber, bringing internet to schools in remote villages, keeping ships connected in the middle of oceans, and giving millions of airline passengers high-speed internet thousands of meters above Earth

It's also helping save lives

From rescuing people stranded in remote wilderness to restoring emergency communications after natural disasters, Starlink is reaching places where traditional networks simply aren't available

What started as Elon standing on a stage in Seattle, describing his vision of a global satellite network, has become the largest satellite constellation humanity has ever built
