Dario Amodei says Anthropic could drive at 200 miles an hour but chooses 90

“So this is one of the central tensions at the heart of the company, but it’s really because this technology is so powerful, it has so many effects in the world. It’s something that every company in this space will have to face, whether they want to or not. We’re just facing it more explicitly and more thoughtfully. And so you think about something like pacing.”

“The idea behind pacing was, imagine that you’re driving your car at 60 miles an hour, and then you discover or invent something that could let you drive the car at 200 miles an hour. This is models building themselves, recursive self-improvement. What we’ve done with pacing is essentially said, look, we could drive at 200 miles an hour. Maybe let’s only drive at 90 miles an hour. Spend time figuring out how to do that safely, and the rest of that surplus, we spend building the model in the right way.”

“And I think in the long run, that will work out pretty well for the investors as well, because the company that builds the safest, that has the trust of the customers and the enterprises of the world, I think that is the company that’s going to be both best for society and, in the long run, I hope, best for the performance of the company.”
