All 26 Starlink V3 satellites launched on Starship Flight 15 are now operational

Those 26 satellites added 26 Tbps of downlink and 4.2 Tbps of uplink capacity to the Starlink network

A typical Falcon 9 V2 Mini launch adds about 2.59 Tbps, so one Starship launch delivered the capacity of 10 Falcon 9 launches

All 4 Starlink launches in October added 10.4 Tbps combined. Flight 15 added 2.5x that in a single flight

Starlink now has 11,210 active satellites delivering 946.7 Tbps of downlink capacity, and Starship is just getting started
